Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Mosselia is designed to respect your privacy. We do not sell your data, and the game contains no advertising.

What we collect

  • Nothing by default. Your progress, coins, farm and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics — only with your consent. If (and only if) you enable “Anonymous statistics”, the app sends anonymous, aggregated usage events (e.g. level started/completed) through Google Firebase Analytics to help us improve the game. It is off by default, you are asked once, and you can change it any time in Settings. These events do not identify you and are never used for advertising or tracking.

Handled by Apple

  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Game Center (leaderboards & achievements) is provided by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy, if you are signed in.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). It contains no ads and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn anonymous statistics on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • contact us via our contact form for any privacy request.
We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.
